Strategic System Objectives
  • Healthcare Accessibility: Eliminates geographical barriers for rural and underserved patients.
  • Preventable Error Elimination: Enforces 99% accuracy in electronic prescription drug matching.
  • HIPAA Security & E2EE: Protects medical data confidentiality with AES-256 encryption.
  • Real-Time Vital Ingestion: Streams wearable heart rate, SpO2, and blood pressure vitals directly to doctor dashboards.
  • 48-Hour Claim Processing: Automated real-time insurance verification and claim tracking.

UI/UX Design System Rules

Mandel's 3 Golden Rules

Place users in control through modeless navigation, reduce memory load via visual recognition over recall, and enforce uniform interface consistency.

Shneiderman's 8 Golden Rules

Informative toast feedback, multi-step closure, inline error prevention, and simple action reversal ("Undo/Back").

Norman's Design Principles

Prominent call-to-action visibility, natural spatial mapping, and affordances designed for healthcare users.
